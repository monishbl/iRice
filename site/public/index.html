  1. Switch to a free console.Press Ctrl Alt F3 and log in.
  2. Start iRice.The check says what's missing, if anything.
    cd ~/iRice && ./demo.sh --check && ./dev.sh tty
  3. Leave when you like.Super Shift Esc ends it. Ctrl Alt F1 takes you back.

New features. Your settings.

iRice checks every few hours. If an update touches something you've changed, you decide before anything installs.

What happens when an update conflicts with my changes?

Open Settings → Software Update. Each conflict waits for your choice:

Settings with new defaults
Keep yours, or take the new default.
Your glass material
Keep your look (saved as Custom), or take the new one.
Shortcut clashes
Keep yours, or take the new shortcut.
Files you edited
Keep both changes when they fit, or only yours, or only the update's.

Settings and shortcuts are backed up before anything changes.

How do I share my look with a friend?

Download the shared look, unpack it, and run ./import.sh --repo ~/iRice. It carries glass, colours, menu bar and dock, and no personal data. Current settings are backed up first.

The glass looks flat, not refractive

The glass plugin didn't build for your Hyprland. Run ~/iRice/tools/setup-tty.sh again after a Hyprland update.

Animations feel choppy on a laptop

Check the display isn't stuck at a low refresh rate. Cool-down lowers it while the laptop is hot, and gives it back after.

The screen froze

From another console (Ctrl Alt F4), run cd ~/iRice && ./dev.sh tty-stop.

How do I remove it?

Delete the ~/iRice folder. Nothing else was changed.
