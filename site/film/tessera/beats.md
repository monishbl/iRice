# Beats

Both versions use the same 8 chapters, the same captured footage and the same titles. They differ in opener, pace and grammar.

## Version A: "Two halves" (calm, split screen), 62 s

- **Shape:** C opening (signature image → product) into D (a chapter tour), bookended so it loops.
- **Grammar:** every chapter is one split frame, Glass on the left and Material on the right, with the same moment playing in both. Its title rises on the seam after the proof. One chapter is cause → effect instead of the same moment twice (circle to search, Quick Share).
- **Continuity agent:** the seam, a thin vertical line of light where the two looks meet, with the Badge riding on it.

## Version B: "Tiles" (fast, alternating), 60 s

- **Shape:** C opening into D, as in A.
- **Grammar:** a kinetic title card (≈1 s) → proof in one look (2.5–4 s), and the look alternates every chapter. The film's own beat is 120 bpm (0.5 s), with cuts on the beat.
- **Continuity agent:** tesserae, small square tiles (glass ones and tonal ones). Every chapter break shatters the frame into tiles that re-form into the next shot.
