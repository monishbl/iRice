# Brief: Tessera launch film

- **Purpose:** the site's hero loop and the launch of the new name.
- **What a viewer should do:** install it (the page carries the install line; the film ends on it before it loops).
- **What they should remember:** *Tessera: one desktop, two looks.*
- **Audience:** Linux and Hyprland users who know tiling window managers and riced dotfiles, and are tired of assembling a desktop from parts. They watch muted, on a landing page, on a laptop or a phone.
- **The sentence:** a Linux desktop usually means choosing between pretty and finished. Tessera is one finished desktop, Glass or Material, and every feature works in both.
- **Proof (the person's picks):**
  1. the look switch;
  2. the island coming alive;
  3. circle to search;
  4. Quick Share;
  5. Live Text;
  6. the AI sidebar and its chats;
  7. dynamic colour;
  8. Quick Settings.
- **Length:** about 60 s, 16:9 master; a 9:16 cut can come later.
- **Sound:** none (muted autoplay); every beat reads without it.
- **Loop:** it ends on its opening image.
- **Brand:**
  - name: Tessera;
  - logo: the Badge (Phosphor circle-wavy, filled);
  - type: Inter in Glass, Google Sans Flex in Material, and the film's own titles in Google Sans Flex (OFL);
  - colour: each look's own; the film's accent is the Badge's.
- **Must-nots:**
  - nothing from the person's real screen, accounts or networks: every take comes from the hidden sandbox with seeded data;
  - no other companies' product names on screen (the project's own rule).
- **Deliverable:** two versions as fully working HTML (no rendered video this round):
  - **A, "Two halves":** two halves meet; calm and premium; split screen, both looks side by side.
  - **B, "Tiles":** tiles become the Badge; energetic; the looks alternate chapter by chapter.
- **Review:** the person rates both.
